FAQ

Common Questions

What are the shelf life and storage requirements? +
Products can be frozen for up to 15 months after production. Once thawed and refrigerated, they must be used within 3 days. After heating, they should be consumed within one meal.
How do you handle delivery and minimum orders? +
We deliver weekly to London via our own frozen logistics, with third-party cold chain coverage across the rest of the UK. European export is available for commercial partners. Get in touch for MOQ details and a tailored quote.
What sales channels are available for B2B partners? +
Our products reach restaurants, caterers, Asian grocers, and distributors across the UK and Europe. We also support wholesale and own-label distribution with retail-ready packaging. Talk to us to find the right fit for your business.
Can you produce custom flavours or private-label products? +
Yes — we welcome OEM and ODM requests. We can design custom flavours and enable large-scale production under your brand. Contact our sales team to get started.
Where can I buy Han Dian products in the UK? +
You can order directly through OOLIOS, our online supermarket, which delivers Taiwanese cuisine to designated UK addresses. Selected products are also available at local Asian supermarkets.
Are Han Dian products available in Europe? +
Han Dian products can be exported to major European cities for commercial partners. For general consumers, we currently ship within the UK mainland only. Please contact our sales team for European distribution enquiries.
